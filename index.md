## ⚙️ How It Works (Without the Tech Jargon)

You might be wondering: *How can a small program on my computer be as smart as a giant AI?*

Here's the secret:

- **Big AIs (like GPT) are generalists.** They know a little about everything, but they're slow and expensive because they're enormous.
- **shrewd is a specialist.** It learns from your examples and focuses exclusively on your one task. It becomes an expert in that narrow field.

Think of it like this: A world-class chef (general AI) can cook any dish, but takes hours and costs a fortune. A local diner cook (shrewd) specializes in one great burger and serves it instantly, for pennies.

Technically, shrewd uses a process called **knowledge distillation** — it takes the wisdom from a large, powerful AI (using tools like LiteLLM) and compresses it into a tiny model that runs locally. It also uses **prompt optimization** to make sure it squeezes every drop of accuracy from your examples.

The result is a model that is:

- **Fast:** Answers in milliseconds, not seconds.
- **Privacy-friendly:** Your data never leaves your computer.
- **Cheap:** No per-use fees. Pay once, use forever.
- **Offline-capable:** Works even without internet.
- **Small:** Takes just a few megabytes of space.
